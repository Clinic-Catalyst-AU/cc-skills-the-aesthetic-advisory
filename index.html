Your Business Brain

Your Google Sheet holds everything about your clinic: your treatments and prices, your team, your offer, and the words your clients use to describe their skin. Claude turns it into one Business Brain file, including how your clients feel and what makes them book, so every ad, email and post starts from the truth about your clinic.

  • Fills your sheet from your website and Instagram, marking anything it isn't sure of for you to check
  • Builds the Business Brain once you have checked the sheet
  • Updates everything when something changes: a new offer, a new price, a new team member
Say: "I've checked my sheet, build my Business Brain." or "I've changed my offer, update everything."

How to get the most out of it

  1. Always work inside your clinic Project, so Claude has your Business Brain and your rules.
  2. Say what you want in plain words. You don't need the skill names.
  3. Change your offer, prices or team? Tell Claude first: "I've changed my offer." It updates everything.
  4. Not happy with something? Tell it what to change, like you would a team member: "warmer", "shorter", "use the word glow".
  5. Videos need the Claude Desktop app. Everything else works in the app or on claude.ai.
